
# DaraaAI

## About Daraa AI ##
Daraa AI is An Intelligent Security System for Detecting Suspicious Behaviors and 
Cyber Attacks Using Artificial Intelligence. 

## Overview ##
Daraa AI is an innovative cybersecurity system that leverages artificial intelligence to 
analyze user and system behaviors in real time, detecting abnormal patterns that may 
indicate potential attacks or malicious activity. 
The system operates locally within the organization’s environment, without relying on 
cloud connections, ensuring data confidentiality and protection. 
The project’s concept is built around developing an AI-driven system that emulates the 
mindset of an ethical hacker—detecting vulnerabilities before they are exploited and 
issuing instant alerts to security teams upon identifying any suspicious behavior. 
While the idea is applicable across multiple sectors—such as healthcare, education, and 
government institutions—the prototype was implemented during the “Amd 
Hackathon”, organized in partnership with Alinma Bank and Tuwaiq Academy. 
During the hackathon, the system was tested in the banking sector using simulated 
financial transaction data to evaluate its capability in detecting fraudulent patterns in 
real time.

## The Problem ##
With the rapid advancement of digital transformation, organizations face growing 
cybersecurity challenges, including: 
• Difficulty in detecting cyberattacks promptly. 
• Weak real-time monitoring of abnormal user behaviors. 
• Dependence on traditional systems that cannot keep up with modern   
attack techniques and speed. 
Proposed Solution 
Dara AI offers an intelligent and proactive cybersecurity solution by: 
• Analyzing digital transactions and behaviors in real time using AI. 
• Automatically detecting suspicious patterns before attackers can exploit 
them. 
• Classifying risk levels as High – Medium – Low. 
• Sending instant alerts to security teams upon detecting potential threats. 
The system functions as an intelligent security assistant that combines the roles of both 
the attacker and the defender, enabling faster detection and response to cyber threats. 
## Algorithms Used ##
The system employs Anomaly Detection techniques in machine learning, primarily 
using: 
1. 
Isolation Forest 
• Used to isolate and detect anomalous behaviors from normal patterns. 
• Efficient in handling and analyzing large datasets quickly. 
2. 
Random Forest 
• Used to classify behaviors and determine risk levels with high accuracy. 
• Relies on aggregating multiple decision trees to achieve precise 
classification (Safe / Suspicious behavior). 
By combining the outputs of both algorithms, Daraa AI provides a comprehensive, real
time analytical model capable of early and accurate threat detection.

## Future Vision ##
In the future, Daraa AI aims to integrate Deep Learning with Threat Intelligence to 
enhance the precision and responsiveness of its detection capabilities. 
The system aspires to expand its protection scope to other critical sectors such as 
healthcare and education, through the development of smart simulation modules that 
can analyze attacker behavior and predict potential vulnerabilities before exploitation. 
Additionally, the project seeks to build an interactive and fully integrated system 
powered by a Large Language Model (LLM Chatbot), serving as an intelligent 
cybersecurity assistant capable of communicating directly with security teams, 
providing instant recommendations and alerts when suspicious activity or a potential 
attack is detected. 
Daraa AI envisions a safer digital future — protected by intelligent algorithms that 
predict and prevent attacks through proactive and innovative defense.
